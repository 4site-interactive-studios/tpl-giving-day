Act

 

Tell Congress: Keep Public Lands in Public Hands

Right now, special interests are pushing to strip protections from our parks, trails, and open spaces—opening the door to development and closing access for future generations. Without strong protections, access to these spaces could disappear.

Congress is considering the bipartisan Public Lands in Public Hands Act, which would prevent the sale or transfer of most federal public lands and ensure they remain open for all. We need your voice.

Take one minute now to keep public lands in public hands by signing the petition, and urging Congress to protect our beloved green spaces!
